What this extension can record

Only numeric video IDs, local timing/status metadata, bounded natural-session interaction counts, and player events. It reads post links to extract the IDs but does not store usernames, captions, messages, credentials, or cookies. If I deliberately start collection from the allowlisted Stanford Qualtrics pilot, that survey receives numeric IDs and its playback log. There is no pilot backend; normal TikTok pages and embeds still communicate with TikTok. During collection, the extension automatically advances and skips covered FYP cards. TikTok may treat those skips or brief watch time as ranking signals, which can influence future recommendations. The separate natural-session survey leaves TikTok visible and records only aggregate pointer-movement bursts, clicks, wheel events, and key-event counts; it never records cursor coordinates or which keys were pressed.
